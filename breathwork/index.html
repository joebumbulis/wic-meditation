Breathwork

A practice you can
follow all the way down.

--:--

Watch it through once before you practice, so you know where it goes. When you're ready, lie down somewhere you can be loud, put something over your eyes, and follow the breath rather than managing it.

Things will move. Your hands may tingle, your jaw may shake, you may find yourself crying without a story attached. None of that is going wrong. Stay with the breath and let it finish what it started.

Give it the full length. The part most people skip is the stillness at the end, and that's where the integration actually happens.

Before you begin

Don't practice while driving, standing, or in water. If you're pregnant, or living with a cardiovascular condition, epilepsy, glaucoma, or significant psychiatric history, talk to your doctor before you try this. Have water nearby, and somewhere soft to land.
